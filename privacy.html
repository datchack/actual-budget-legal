Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

1. Purpose

This application is a personal installation of Actual Budget used solely for managing the owner's personal finances and personal bank account information.

2. Personal data

The application may process financial information obtained from the owner's personal bank account, including account information, transaction information, balances, dates, amounts and transaction descriptions.

3. Use of financial information

Financial information is used exclusively by the owner for personal budgeting, accounting, financial tracking and transaction management. The information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising purposes.

4. Bank connection

Bank account access is provided through Enable Banking and the applicable banking institution. Access is authorized by the account owner.

5. Data storage

Actual Budget is operated as a self-hosted installation on the owner's personal computer. Financial data is intended to remain under the owner's control.

6. Data retention

Personal financial data is retained only for as long as necessary for the owner's personal financial management. The owner may delete the data from the application at any time.

7. Third-party services

Enable Banking may process information as necessary to provide bank account access and account information services. Such processing is subject to Enable Banking's own terms and privacy documentation.
